Before contacting us

  1. Update Explore China and iOS to the latest available versions.
  2. Check your internet connection before opening maps, booking pages, or other external links.
  3. Close and reopen the app if a screen stops responding.

What to include in a report

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • Your Explore China app version
  • The steps that caused the issue
  • A screenshot, if it contains no sensitive information

Never email passport numbers, bank card details, passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive information.

About Explore China support

Explore China provides general travel information and planning tools. Support cannot provide legal, immigration, financial, medical, booking, or payment advice.
