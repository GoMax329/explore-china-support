Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Explore China iOS app handles information.

Who operates the app

Explore China is operated by Yang Mingxi. Questions about this policy can be sent to explorechinasupport@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Explore China does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, passport details, bank card details, or payment information.

The app does not use advertising identifiers, behavioral advertising, or cross-app tracking. The operator does not receive analytics or usage data from the current version of the app.

Information stored on your device

Saved places and trip plans are stored locally on your device so the app can remember your choices. This information is not transmitted to the operator.

You can remove saved items and trip entries inside the app. Uninstalling Explore China removes its locally stored data from that device.

Third-party services and links

At your request, Explore China can open App Store pages, map apps, government websites, booking providers, and other external services. Loading or opening an external service may allow that provider to process technical information such as your IP address or device information under its own privacy policy.

Explore China does not control third-party services. Review their privacy policies before providing them with personal or payment information.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Explore China does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Because the operator does not receive your locally stored trip data, the operator does not retain it on a server.

If you contact support by email, your email provider and ours process the message. We use support correspondence only to respond, investigate issues, and improve the app. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

Children's privacy

Explore China is a general travel reference app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information handled by the app. No method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
